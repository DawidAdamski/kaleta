# Fidelity report - 1f Dashboard, phone

Spec: [`artboards/1f.html`](../artboards/1f.html). Pictures: `.fidelity/1f/index.html` after `shoot 1f`.

Read at 390x844, seeded demo ledger. Artboard values are the inline ones in
`1f.html`; app values are `getComputedStyle` off the running page.

Two things shape this report. The plan's Scope says **the phone shell is
unchanged** in this pass - the tab bar and the drawer behind "More" are
`restyle-dashboard-mobile`'s, and the rows below that compare them are
recorded, not repaired. And `1f` is drawn inside a phone frame: a rounded
390px card with a 9:41 status bar and a home-indicator gutter, none of which
a browser page has.

Four rows below are structural rather than a value: **2** (one header serves
both widths, dropping what does not fit) and **10**, **11** and **13**
(renderings a widget cannot choose, deferred to
[`restyle-fidelity-phone-widgets`](../../../plans/restyle-fidelity-phone-widgets.md)).
They are what the plan's `[owner]` criterion is for.
